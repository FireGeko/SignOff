Where do I get a logo link?

Email signatures can't carry uploaded pictures reliably, so your logo has to live on the web. Two easy ways:

  1. From your own website. Open your site, right-click your logo and choose Copy image address. Paste it above.
  2. Upload it somewhere public. Any host that gives you a direct link ending in .png or .jpg works. Ask whoever runs your website to add it if you can.

Avoid OneDrive, Google Drive and Dropbox share links. They open a viewer page, not the image, so the logo shows as broken.

For a sharp result, use a PNG about twice as wide as it will display.

Add it to your email

  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Outlook, open Settings (the gear icon) → Accounts → Signatures.
  3. Click + New signature, give it a name, click in the box and press Ctrl+V (⌘+V on Mac).
  4. Under Select default signatures, choose it for new messages and for replies.
  5. Click Save.
  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Outlook, go to File → Options → Mail → Signatures…
  3. Click New, name it, then click in the edit box and press Ctrl+V.
  4. On the right, choose it for New messages and Replies/forwards.
  5. Click OK.
  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Gmail, click the gear icon → See all settings.
  3. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click + Create new.
  4. Name it, click in the box and paste.
  5. Set it as the default for new emails and replies, then click Save Changes at the bottom of the page.
  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Mail, open Settings → Signatures.
  3. Choose your account, click +, and untick Always match my default message font.
  4. Click in the right-hand box, delete the placeholder text and paste.
  5. Close the window. The signature saves automatically.

Logo not showing after you paste? Check the logo link in step 4. Your email app may also ask you to “show images” the first time.
